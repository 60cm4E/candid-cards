Privacy Policy

Candid Cards — Last updated: May 1, 2026

Overview

Candid Cards ("the App") is a conversation card application for couples. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data.

The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration. All app data is stored locally on your device.

Local Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Saved (favorited) conversation questions
  • Unlocked pack information
  • Sound preference settings
  • Daily question tracking

This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

In-App Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases to unlock premium card packs. These purchases are processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. We do not have access to your payment information.

Advertisements

The App uses Google AdMob to display optional rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing — for processing in-app purchases
  • Google AdMob — for optional rewarded advertisements

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

## Full Description (English)

Candid Cards is a beautifully designed conversation card app for couples who want to move beyond small talk and truly connect.

Each deck is crafted with thoughtful questions and follow-up prompts that guide meaningful dialogue — from playful icebreakers to profound emotional exploration.

🃏 HOW IT WORKS
• Choose a card pack that matches your mood
• Swipe through beautifully designed question cards
• Use follow-up prompts to go even deeper
• Save your favorite questions for later

💕 WHAT'S INCLUDED
• Couple Starter (FREE) — 20 fun & light questions to spark connection
• Deeper Us — Vulnerable questions for deeper emotional intimacy
• Date Night — Playful prompts to break routines and create memories
• Future & Values — Big-picture questions about life direction
• Family Bonds — Explore family dynamics and how they shape your love
• Self Discovery — Know yourself and your partner on a whole new level

✨ FEATURES
• 105+ handcrafted conversation cards
• Follow-up prompts for richer dialogue
• Save & revisit your favorite questions
• Daily free question
• Beautiful dark mode design
• Haptic feedback & ambient sounds
• Works offline — no internet required

Perfect for date nights, road trips, quiet evenings, or anytime you want to feel closer to the person you love.

Download Candid Cards and start the conversation that matters.
